# Guitar Practice Helper

A single-page practice tool for guitar and piano: it turns a description of an exercise into notation, tab and playback, and keeps a record of what has been practised.

## Language

**Exercise spec**:
The complete description of one exercise — what to play, in what key, in what shape — independent of how it is displayed or stored.
_Avoid_: settings, params, config, selection

**Exercise**:
The realised notes of an exercise spec in one key: the pitches, their fingering and the name that labels them.
_Avoid_: drill, pattern
